👋 Hi! I’m a PhD student in the [Evolutionary Intelligence](https://www.cwi.nl/en/groups/evolutionary-intelligence/) group at the Dutch Institute for Maths & Computer Science (CWI).

My primary research interests are **Hyperparameter Optimization** and **Synthetic data**. I believe leveraging **Evolutionary Algorithms** (EAs) for non-differentiable Machine Learning problems such as these is a potentially high-impact research direction. In addition to not requiring gradients, most EAs are trivial to parallelize & scale, so they are fully compatible with [Sutton’s Bitter Lesson](http://www.incompleteideas.net/IncIdeas/BitterLesson.html).

Life-wise I’m into fantasy, hiking, and discussing weird ideas.

Always happy to chat about these topics! Drop me a line at [alex@chebykin.dev](mailto:alex@chebykin.dev)
